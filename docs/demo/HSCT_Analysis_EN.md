# Howe Sound Crest Trail: single-day traverse

You completed the Cypress Lodge to Highway 99 traverse on 27 September 2026: 33.91 km in 14:30:48, with about 2,167 m of ascent and 2,974 m of descent. The defining demand was combining repeated climbs, technical ridge travel and a long descent in one day.

BC Parks describes the HSCT as an especially difficult backcountry route, with exposed ridgelines, scree slopes and cliffs. It continues past the Lions and Deeks Lake before descending to Highway 99.[1][2] This is a traverse where reaching the high ground still leaves substantial work ahead. Your highest difficulty ratings for both climbing and descending fit that character. With nearly 3,000 m of recorded descent, the downhill was a major part of the day's workload, not just the exit after the climbs.

You felt rested after eight hours of good-quality sleep and reported sunny, comfortable conditions at 13°C. The hike was nevertheless at the top of your effort and technicality scales. For future route choices, treat this as a reference for a demanding technical day, rather than assuming another hike of similar distance will feel similar.

For planning another long traverse, use the full 14:30:48, including breaks, as this outing's time reference. You explicitly marked it as representative of your own pace. TrailSense's moving and stopped times are algorithm estimates, not measured watch pauses, so they should not become a fixed break allowance. One outing is a useful reference, not a reliable forecast. Reserve time and energy for the descent, allow a margin for conditions, and keep the following day flexible for rest.

You finished the full HSCT in a single day and gave the experience your highest enjoyment rating. A demanding day, and one you were glad to have done.
